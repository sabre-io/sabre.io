[KashCal](https://github.com/KashCal/KashCal) is an open-source (Apache-2.0)
calendar app for Android with CalDAV and CardDAV support. It works with
sabre/dav-based servers, including [Baïkal](https://sabre.io/baikal/).

* Source: [https://github.com/KashCal/KashCal](https://github.com/KashCal/KashCal)
* F-Droid: [https://f-droid.org/packages/org.onekash.kashcal](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.onekash.kashcal)
